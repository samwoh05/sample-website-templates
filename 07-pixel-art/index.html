Stage 01 / About

Nine years of drawing small

I started making sprites for a friend's jam game in 2017. I never stopped. Today I work with indie studios on characters, tilesets, menus and the small animations that make a game feel good under the thumb.

I work at native resolution and scale up at the end. I place every pixel by hand. I write palette notes for every project so the next artist on the team can pick it up on day one.
