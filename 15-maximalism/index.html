No.2 THE LIBRARY

2About the woman who owns 61 yellows

I grew up above my grandmother's haberdashery in Chiaia, where ribbons were sorted by mood and buttons by temperament. I trained in textile design in Como, then spent four years apprenticed to a scenic painter, learning to make a cardboard wall pass for marble from the back row.

Casa Plum opened in 2014 with one screen-printing table and a parrot. Today the studio draws wallpaper, weaves textiles, paints plates and dresses whole rooms for hotels, bars, stages and very brave homes.

I believe beige is a decision you can undo. Every project starts with too much, gets edited with care, then gets a little more back.
