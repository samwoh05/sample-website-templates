Casa Azulejo Reading RoomPorto

Six years lighting stages, then libraries.

Stage work taught me to light for how a room feels.

I start every project by sitting in the room at the hour people will use it. A library at 5pm on a grey Tuesday is a different building from the drawings.

Most of my clients are architects. I join at concept, write the colour and light brief, and stay through commissioning, because a scene only exists once someone has tuned it in the space.

What I do

Four parts of a project. Most clients hire me for two or three.

  • 01

    Colour strategy

    A palette written for daylight, not for a screen.

    • Colour plan
    • Sample boards
    • Finish schedule

  • 02

    Light scenes

    Four to six scenes per room, named for what happens in them.

    • Scene matrix
    • Fixture brief
    • Control notes

  • 03

    Glass and material spec

    Which surfaces reflect, which absorb, which glow.

    • Material samples
    • Reflectance targets

  • 04

    Site calibration

    I stay until the dimmers are right, usually three nights.

    • Aim and focus sessions
    • Final scene record
