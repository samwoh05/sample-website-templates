01About

I design printed and digital matter that people can read from across a street.

I run a small studio in Basel with one other designer. Most of our work is for concert halls, museums, archives and publishers. We start with the grid and the text, then add image and colour only where they carry information.

I have taught typography one day a week since 2017. Teaching keeps the work honest. If a student cannot see why a line breaks where it does, the line is probably wrong.
