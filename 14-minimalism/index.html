01  About

Signs are the last thing anyone wants to read

I plan how people find their way through hospitals, stations and museums, then draw the signs that help them do it. Fewer signs, placed better, almost always work.

Every project starts on foot. I walk the building with the people who run it, once at 7 in the morning and once at 11 at night, because a corridor at shift change is a different place from the same corridor when it is empty.

I write the words on the signs before I draw anything, then test printed mockups on site with tape and a ladder. Most of the work is deciding what not to say. The studio is three people, and every project has one of us on it from the first walk to the last installed sign.

02  Work

Five projects

  1. 01

    Sint Aldric Hospital

    Strategy, sign family, 1,400 signs Strategy, Signage 2024

    Strategy, Signage

    An 11-storey teaching hospital where patients arrive stressed and staff move fast. We renamed every department by floor and colour band, then cut the sign count by a quarter.

    Scope

    • Route study
    • Naming and numbering
    • 1,400 signs
    • Fabrication drawings
  2. 02

    Veerhaven tram line

    Stop identity and signs for 14 stops Identity, Signage 2023

    Identity, Signage

    A new tram line through old streets with little room for poles. Each stop got one totem and one canopy band that carry everything a rider needs.

    Scope

    • Stop identity
    • Totem and canopy
    • Timetable panels
    • 14 stops
  3. 03

    Hollow objects

    Exhibition graphics, Museum Brakel Exhibition 2022

    Exhibition

    Six rooms of empty vessels and casts. Room texts were held to 80 words so visitors could read them standing in front of the work.

    Scope

    • Room texts
    • Object labels
    • Entrance wall
    • 6 rooms
  4. 04

    Lindeholm University Library

    Signage and floor numbers across 6 floors Signage 2021

    Signage

    Students kept getting lost between two stair cores. One large floor number per landing, cut into the wall, fixed most of it.

    Scope

    • Floor-number system
    • Shelf signs
    • Stair cores
    • 6 floors
  5. 05

03  Services

What I can do for a building

01 Wayfinding strategy

02 Sign families
Drawn and specified sign types, ready for fabricators to quote and build.
Typically 8 to 14 weeks
03 Exhibition graphics
Room texts, labels and object captions that people actually read standing up.
Typically 4 to 8 weeks
04 Sign typefaces
Custom or adapted letters for long distances, low light and bad angles.
Typically 12 weeks and up
