Side A / 00 / Inner sleeve

Twelve years of opening titles

I started cutting idents for a Lisbon music channel in 2014 and never stopped working at night. Today I direct title sequences, broadcast packages and the odd music video, usually as a studio of one with a composer and a 3D artist I have trusted for years.

Every job runs the same way. Storyboards first, then one animatic review where we argue about pacing, then a locked schedule. You see moving frames early and nothing changes in the last week unless you ask.
