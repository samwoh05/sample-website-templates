Ines Moriya · Ceramics and objects · Porto

Made by hand. Mended in plain sight.

Stoneware, ash glaze and wood smoke. I make bowls, tableware and light for people who keep things a long time, and I repair what breaks with lacquer and gold.

ii.

Clay keeps every hand that held it.

I trained as a potter in Mashiko and came home to Porto in 2017. The studio is a former cork warehouse in Campanhã: two wheels, a gas kiln, and a share in a wood-fired kiln in the hills near Amarante that we fire twice a year.

Most of what I make is meant to be used every day. When it chips, bring it back. A repair with urushi and gold takes a few weeks, and the bowl comes home with its history showing.

    By edition

    Small weekend workshops at the studio on throwing, ash glazes and repair. Six people at most.
