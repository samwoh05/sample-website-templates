Selected work

Packs, posters, sleeves, signs and one typeface of my own.

  1. Fizzbank Soda Co.

    Fizzbank can range on a shelf - 1600x1000

    Packaging 2025

    Six cans, one loud family

    A Stockport soda maker had six flavours and no shelf presence. I gave every can the same huge wordmark and its own colour slab. Lined up, the range reads as one block from the end of the aisle.

    • Can range
    • Multipack sleeve
    • Shelf strips
    Open case study: Fizzbank Soda Co.

  2. Late Shift Market

    Late Shift Market poster wall - 1600x1000

    Posters 2023 to 2025

    A new poster every Friday

    A night food market in Salford needed a fresh poster each week that still looked like the same market. One grid, one typeface, a new colour pair every Friday, printed on the studio Riso.

    • Weekly posters
    • Riso printing
    • Stall signs
    Open case study: Late Shift Market

  3. Plumbline Records

    Plumbline record sleeves - 1600x1000

    Identity 2024

    Sleeves you can spot in a crate

    A small Leeds label with a big back catalogue. The sleeve system uses a thick black frame, the catalogue number at poster size and one spot colour per genre.

    • Sleeve system
    • Label wordmark
    • Tour posters
    Open case study: Plumbline Records

  4. Kettleworth Library

    Kettleworth Library floor sign - 1600x1000

    Wayfinding 2024

    Signs a seven-year-old can read

    The town library moved into an old cotton mill. I drew signs with huge floor numbers, plain words and one colour per floor, then tested them with the after-school club.

    • Floor maps
    • Pictograms
    Open case study: Kettleworth Library

About Bea

Contents: one designer. Packed in Manchester.

Portrait of Bea at the Riso - 800x1000

I'm a graphic designer in Manchester. I started out screen printing gig posters in a shared studio, and I still think at poster size.

Now I design packaging, posters and type for small food, drink and music brands. Most of them have a small budget and a crowded shelf. My job is to make them hard to miss.

I work alone with a printer I trust. You deal with me from the first call to the last proof.
