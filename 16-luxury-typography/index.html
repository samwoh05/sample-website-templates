Planche II

A bench, a burin and fifteen years of patience.

I trained as a hand engraver in the Vallée de Joux and opened this atelier in Geneva in 2011. Most of my work begins with a single letter: the initial a house will stamp, emboss or cut into metal for the next fifty years. I draw it in pencil, cut it in steel, proof it on cotton paper and only then build the identity around it.

The houses I work with sell perfume, jewels, time and discretion. They rarely want to be noticed; they want to be recognised. That is a typographic problem, and I treat it as one: optical sizes for every scale, true small caps, figures that sit in the line, and letterforms that survive being cut at four millimetres.

  • 15 years at the bench
  • 41 houses served
  • 3 working languages
