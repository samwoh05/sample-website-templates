What you can hire me for

Four ways to work together. Most projects mix two of them.

Brand identity

Logos, type and colour systems that hold up on a van and on a favicon.

You get: Logo suite, guidelines, launch assets.

Packaging

Structural ideas and printed surfaces, tested as real mockups before print.

You get: Dielines, 3D mockups, print files.

3D characters

Mascots built to move, with poses and expressions your team can reuse.

You get: Model, pose library, turnaround sheet.

Motion for social

Short loops that make characters and products feel alive.

You get: 6 to 15 second loops and cutdowns.
