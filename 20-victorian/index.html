About the printer

When the old Calder Works print shop closed in 2014, its type went to auction. I bought 41 cases of wood letter, a composing frame and an Albion hand press, and moved them into a stone shed by the canal. The sign over the door still reads Wrenfield & Daughter, which was my grandfather's joke and is now mine.

I letter, set and print show bills, playbills, labels and shop fascias for fairs, music halls, bands and small makers. Every job is set by hand and proofed on the press before a full run, so you see the real ink on the real paper before you agree to it.

Most commissions take three to six weeks. I take on around twenty a year.
