The studio

Small rooms, wrong weather.

I build sets that look like a calm dream. One table. One lamp. The rain is only falling on the bread.

I studied sculpture in Porto and spent six years making props for theatre. Now I build still life sets for photographers, brands and publishers. Everything is made by hand and shot in camera. I work with a small crew of carpenters and painters in a warehouse near the river in Marvila.

Most projects start with one wrong detail. A tap that pours sand. A key the size of a chair. I keep the rest quiet so the detail can do the work.

What I make.

Four ways to work together. Most clients book a full set.

  1. Set design.

    Full sets for photo and film, from sketch to build to strike. I bring the crew.

  2. Still life art direction.

    I direct the objects on shoot day. Placement, scale, light, the one thing that is wrong.

  3. Props and miniatures.

    Handmade props at any scale. Oversized keys, tiny chairs, taps that pour sand.

  4. Concepts for campaigns.

    A short book of sketches and references before anything is built. Good for pitches.
