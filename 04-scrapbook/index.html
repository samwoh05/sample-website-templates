Who's holding the pen

I grew up above my parents' café in Bonfim, drawing customers on the backs of order slips.

I studied illustration in Porto and drew for a newspaper for five years. Now I work for myself, for restaurants, publishers, towns and festivals.

I draw on location whenever I can. A map is better when I've walked it. A menu is better when I've eaten from it.

I work alone from a shared studio near the river. You talk to me from the first sketch to the final file.

What I can draw for you

How a job runs

  1. Walk it. We meet where the drawing will live.

  2. Sketch it. Pencil roughs and two rounds of changes.

  3. Ink it. Final art in ink and watercolour.

  4. Hand it over. Print files, web files and the originals if you want them.
