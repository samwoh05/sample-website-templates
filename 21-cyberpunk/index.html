02 // TENANCY NOTICE

The light on your block

営業

I have lit Sector Vell for eleven years. I started bending real neon for noodle bars on Sunken Row, and I now build signage systems that run on LED, projection and holographic film. Most of my work goes up after dark and has to be readable through rain, steam and traffic.

I work with the people who use the street, not only the people who pay for it. Before I draw anything I stand on the corner at 02:00 and watch where people look. Then I design signs in up to four scripts that work at walking pace and at tram speed.
