Sheet 04/06

Process and services

How a job runs

stages can be booked alone

most clients start here

  1. 1-2 wks

    01 Concept sketching

    Forty-plus sketches, then three directions on one sheet each. You pick, or we mix.

    Typical: 1-2 wks

  2. 2-3 wks

    02 Form and CAD

    Surface model, GA drawings, and renders good enough for a buyer meeting.

    Typical: 2-3 wks

  3. 3-5 wks

    03 Prototypes

    Foam first, then print, then a first-off from the supplier. Each one gets marked up.

    Typical: 3-5 wks

  4. 1-4 wks

    04 Production support

    I go to the factory. Tolerances, finishes, sample sign-off.

    Typical: 1-4 wks

6-14 weeks

A typical job takes 6 to 14 weeks in total.

  • Concept sketching
  • Form development
  • Prototyping
  • CMF
  • Design for manufacture
  • Supplier visits
