Profile

She works when the port does

Noor Halvorsen photographs night work in and around Rotterdam: crane crews, bakers, river pilots, ferry regulars. She also edits other photographers’ pictures for print.

Words by the editors | Photographs by Noor Halvorsen

Most of Noor Halvorsen’s photographs are made between 22:00 and 06:00. She started working nights in 2019 on a three-week commission at the Kade 7 terminal in Waalhaven, and stayed for fourteen months.

Her method is slow. She goes back to the same place until people stop noticing the camera, then keeps going. A bakery took a year. A ferry line took sixty crossings, one portrait each.

The other half of her week goes on other people’s pictures. As picture editor at Delta Weekly she chose and sequenced photographs for 38 issues, working to the page and to the deadline.

She lives in Rotterdam-Zuid, a ten-minute cycle from the terminal gates, and still keeps a night bag packed by the door.

Feature 4.1 | Port

Night Shift, Waalhaven

Fourteen months with the crane crews of the Kade 7 terminal, photographed between 22:00 and 06:00.

2019–2020 | Client: Harbour Office Quarterly, Kade 7 Terminal | 22 pages

The night crew at Kade 7 is eleven people. They move about 900 containers a shift. Halvorsen rode in the crane cabins, ate in the canteen at 02:00 and photographed the handovers at six, when one crew passes the terminal to the next.

The commission was for four pictures and a cover. The terminal manager let her stay once the crews started asking for prints of themselves. By the second winter she had a locker in the canteen and a hard hat with her name on it.

The finished story ran over 22 pages in Harbour Office Quarterly. It is mostly hands, cabins and the yellow light of the yard, with very few wide shots of the cranes.

Feature 4.2 | Family

The Bakers of Spoorstraat

The Vellinga bakery’s last year before closing, from the first oven at 03:30 to the final loaf.

2022 | Published in Delta Weekly | 18 pictures

The Vellingas baked on Spoorstraat for 61 years. In 2022 the lease ended and nobody in the family wanted to take it on.

Halvorsen arrived at half past three most mornings that year. The series ends on the last Saturday, with the shelves empty by nine.
